Honest pros and cons

Why people like it

  • Free. No subscription, no paywalled cosmetics, no ads.
  • Private calls. Voice and video go directly between people, not through our servers.
  • Deep customization of profiles and the whole app.
  • Every platform, plus a full web app with nothing to install.
  • Updates itself on Windows, macOS, Linux and Android.
  • Small and fast: the Windows app is about 8 MB.

What to know

  • Big group calls (more than about 6 people) are heavier, because calls are peer-to-peer.
  • iPhone/iPad installs by sideloading (SideStore, AltStore or Sideloadly), not the App Store yet.
  • macOS builds aren't notarised by Apple, so the first launch needs right-click → Open.
  • Young community: fewer ready-made bots and integrations than older apps.
  • Push notifications on phones work in the web app and Android, not the iPhone build.
