Privacy, in plain words

Aether is a small, free app made for friends and family. We don't sell data, show ads, or track you around the web.

Last updated 17 November 2026.

What we store

  • Your account: email, username, profile (name, avatar, bio, decorations) and settings.
  • What you send: messages, files, Moments, lists, games and reactions, so the people you share them with can see them. In end-to-end encrypted chats we only store ciphertext we can't read.
  • Your connections: friends, servers you're in, blocks and reports.
  • Activity you choose to share: your status, the game you're playing and play history (if game activity is on), and what you're listening to on Spotify (if turned on).
  • Birthday, if you add one: month and day only, never the year. Only friends see it, and only if you allow it.
  • Error and bug reports: what went wrong, the app version and platform, and anything you write or attach to a report.
  • Contact form messages: your name, email and message, kept for 180 days.

What stays on your device

  • Calls: voice, video and screen sharing go directly between participants and are encrypted. They don't pass through our servers.
  • Translation and voice transcripts run on your device. The language model downloads once from Hugging Face; your messages never leave your device for this.
  • Game detection looks at your installed games and running programs on your computer. Only the name of the game you're playing is shared, and only while game activity is on.
  • Encryption keys for end-to-end encrypted chats.

How long things last

  • Disappearing messages are deleted when their timer runs out.
  • Moments disappear for everyone else after 24 hours, and you can delete yours any time.
  • Play sessions are deleted after 90 days; mini-games after a week of no moves.
  • Accounts that never confirmed their email are removed after 7 days.
  • Everything else stays until you delete it or delete your account.

Who we share data with

Only the services that run Aether: Supabase (database, sign-in and file storage), Vercel (this website and the web app), GitHub (app downloads and updates), Cloudflare Turnstile (stops bots at sign-up and on the contact form), and Google (sending emails, like the contact form). GIF searches go through our server, and the GIFs themselves load from Giphy. Link previews are fetched by our server so your IP isn't shared with the website. We never sell your data.

Your controls

  • Download my data and Delete account in Settings → My Account.
  • Who can message you, add you to groups and find you: Settings → Privacy & Safety.
  • Game activity, play history and Spotify status: Settings → Activity Privacy.
  • Moments audiences and close friends, birthday visibility: Settings → Privacy & Safety.
  • Signed-in devices, two-factor sign-in, passkeys and backup codes: Settings → My Account.
